Reflection

Creating this web-based educational portfolio allowed me to showcase my skills in web development, organization, and professional presentation. The goal was to design a clean, easy-to-navigate site that highlights my projects, certifications, and growth as a Software Development student.

Throughout this project, I strengthened my understanding of HTML and CSS by building multi-page layouts, implementing responsive design, and maintaining consistent styling across all pages. For example, I used reusable CSS classes to ensure visual consistency, which improved both usability and overall design quality

One of the most valuable parts of this process was thinking from an employer’s perspective. I focused on presenting my projects clearly and professionally. In the future, I plan to continue improving this portfolio by adding more visuals, refining design elements, and integrating more advanced features.

Overall, this portfolio reflects not only my technical skills, but also my growth, persistence, and commitment to continuous learning.
