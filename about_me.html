About Me

Personal Philosophy

After high school, many people are expected to already know what they want to major in. Some choose nursing, others law enforcement, and some feel called to serve our country. For me, that decision began much earlier. In elementary school, I took a coding class, and that was the moment I realized computers were truly my passion.

As I continued through high school, that early curiosity grew into clarity. Exploring programming more deeply confirmed that Software Development was the path I wanted to pursue. It aligned with my strengths, interests, and long-term goals.

As a Latina woman in technology, I hope to inspire and encourage others to pursue this field. Software development is not limited to one group — women belong here, and we can absolutely thrive.

Professional Goals

This portfolio highlights the projects I have completed throughout my academic journey. Each one reflects growth, persistence, and the technical skills I continue to develop. My goal is to build innovative, user-focused applications while continuously learning and expanding my expertise in software development.
